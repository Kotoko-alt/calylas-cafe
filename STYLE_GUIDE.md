# Calyla's Café Style Guide

## Brand Identity

- Brand name: **Calyla's Café**
- Tone: warm, cozy, modern local coffee shop
- Mood: earthy, comforting, handcrafted

## Color Palette

- **Primary**: #6B4226 (Dark Coffee Brown)
- **Secondary**: #D9A15A (Warm Gold)
- **Accent**: #F5E1B8 (Cream)
- **Background**: #1D1308 (Deep Espresso)
- **Text Dark**: #2C1A0F
- **Text Light**: #FFFFFF

## Typography

- **Heading**: Playfair Display, serif
- **Body**: Inter, sans-serif
- **Accent text**: small uppercase spacing for buttons and labels
- Example sizes:
  - H1: 4rem
  - H2: 2.5rem
  - Body: 1rem
  - Button: 0.9rem

## Spacing

- Use generous spacing and padding for a relaxed, premium feel
- Card padding: 1.5rem to 2rem
- Section spacing: 4rem to 6rem
- Border radius: 16px to 32px

## Components

### Hero section
- Background: dark photo overlay or warm gradient
- CTA buttons: rounded pill shape, one solid gold and one ghost white
- Supporting text: light, soft tone with high contrast

### Quote card / feature card
- Background: deep brown gradient with subtle overlay
- Text: ivory or cream for readability
- Icon: coffee cup emoji or simple accent line

### Menu cards
- Rounded corners and subtle shadows
- Image top, item details below
- Hover effect: slight lift and stronger shadow

### Buttons
- Primary button: gold fill, dark text, rounded full
- Secondary button: transparent white border, light text
- Hover state: slightly brighter background or stronger border

## Image style

- Use rich, warm food and coffee imagery
- Prefer images with natural wood, ceramic, and warm lighting
- Crop to highlight the product, not the background

## Usage

- Keep the layout clean and easy to scan
- Use cards for menu items with clear category chips
- Use contrast between dark backgrounds and cream text
- Keep the design consistent across the homepage and admin pages
